## The problem

I took over the IFC portfolio after several project managers had left. Knowledge transfer was weak, delivery and production support were unstable, and production deployments were ad hoc.

## What I did

- Took ownership, and stabilised delivery and customer confidence in about 3 months.
- Ran both development and production support across 50+ applications.
- Replaced ad-hoc deployments with implementation windows: about every week at first, later about every two weeks.
- Set up a weekly management review with 6–7 client managers.
- Introduced Remedy-based visibility of ticket ageing and SLA tracking, with a strong focus on timely resolution of production incidents.
- Tightened testing and release discipline.

## Outcome

- A predictable release cadence, with priority tickets handled on time.
- Operational visibility the client and the team shared.
- No contractual SLA breaches or payment penalties across the engagement.
- The portfolio expanded to further engagements.
- 2013 Project of the Year: Cognizant internal recognition for the scale, complexity and delivery performance of the IFC portfolio.
